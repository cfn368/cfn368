### Hi there 👋

MSc Economics at the University of Copenhagen, analyst at [Erhvervslivets Tænketank](https://www.e-tank.dk/).

I build models, be it industrial organisation, energy systems, market design. Mostly in Python. Most of what's here started as either a paper I was writing or a task I got tired of doing by hand.

**Papers and projects**

- [Human Capitalists in the Intangible Era](https://github.com/cfn368/ba_capinc) — two-sector GE model of welfare incidence as the labour/capital line dissolves. My BSc thesis, in Python.
- [Datacenter on-site power: SMR vs. solar+wind+battery](https://github.com/cfn368/on_site_datacenter_mdl) — LP cost optimisation under an hourly on-site share requirement.
- [Renewable energy, weather variation and electricity prices](https://github.com/cfn368/RE-n-the-weather) — counterfactual prognosis of future price volatility.
- [Nuclear power in Denmark's energy mix](https://github.com/cfn368/EnergyPLAN_analysis) — EnergyPLAN cost analysis, 1 GW nuclear vs. offshore wind, against Klimafremskrivning 2026.
- - [Electrification in the EU](https://github.com/cfn368/electrification_EU) — shift-share decomposition of electrification rates. How much is sectors getting cleaner, how much is just the mix shifting? Runs on live Eurostat data.
